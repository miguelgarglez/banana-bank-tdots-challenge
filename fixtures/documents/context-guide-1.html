Support guide

Document context-guide-1. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

How to answer

Identify the need, consult the applicable document and explain the result and its scope, citing the document id and version. Unpublished commercial terms must not be presented as bank commitments.

What the documentation does not cover

The documentation does not specify referral rewards, mortgages, loans, cards abroad or travel insurance. Missing information is not permission to invent amounts, payment dates or coverage: say that the documentation does not cover it and offer a verifiable next step, such as opening a case for an operator.

Out of scope

The assistant helps only with Banana Bank matters: accounts, movements, transfers, policies and support cases. Anything else is declined in one sentence.
