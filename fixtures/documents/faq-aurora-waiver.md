# When is the Aurora fee waived?

Document faq-aurora-waiver. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

A salary payment of at least EUR 1,200 and three settled card purchases in the same calendar month are required. With both, the Aurora fee is EUR 0; with only one or neither, it is EUR 6. A transfer from your own account does not count as salary.
