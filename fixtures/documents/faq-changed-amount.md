# Can I change an amount after confirmation?

Document faq-changed-amount. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

Changing source, destination, amount or description invalidates the previous confirmation. Review and authorize the new details as a new proposal.
