# What if I do not have enough money?

Document faq-insufficient-funds. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

The bank rejects the operation and records neither debit nor credit. A rejection does not create debt in the account.
