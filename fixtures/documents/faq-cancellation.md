# Can I cancel a completed operation?

Document faq-cancellation. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

Closing the chat or changing screens does not reverse a completed operation. An operator must check its status in the ledger and explain the available options.
