# How do I authorize a transfer?

Document faq-confirmation. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

First review the summary with source, destination, amount and description; then confirm that exact summary, either with the confirmation button or by replying with the confirmation code shown in the proposal. Asking what it would cost, preparing a draft or mentioning an amount does not confirm anything. A proposal expires after ten minutes.
