# Can someone else see my chat?

Document faq-personal-data. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

Conversations and operations belong to the customer. Switching the person selector loads that person's context without mixing histories. Operators see cases and their evidence, but cannot move another person's funds.
