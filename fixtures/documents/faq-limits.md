# What is the maximum amount?

Document faq-limits. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

A transfer can be up to EUR 100,000, subject to available funds and authorization. Negative amounts and fractions smaller than one cent are not allowed.
