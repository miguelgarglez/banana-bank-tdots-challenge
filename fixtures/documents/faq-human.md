# Can I ask for human support?

Document faq-human. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

Yes. Ask to open a case for an operator; the assistant opens it only when you agree. A case does not automatically reverse or repeat transactions.
