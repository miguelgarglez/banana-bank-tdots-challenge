# Aurora · archived conditions

Document archive-aurora-1. Version 1. Effective from 2026-01-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document. Historical document: valid from 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-31. It does not describe current terms.

## Terms during this period

The Aurora fee during this period was EUR 8 per month, without the later waiver. The salary waiver was introduced on 2026-09-01 and did not exist in this period. Internal transfers between simulator accounts had no fee and the limit per transfer was EUR 100,000.

## How to use this document

Use it only to answer questions about a date inside its validity period, and say explicitly that the terms are historical. For the current fee, see Aurora · fees and conditions (aurora-fees-2026). Combining amounts from different versions can lead a customer to an incorrect decision.
