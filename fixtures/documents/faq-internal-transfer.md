# What does an internal transfer cost?

Document faq-internal-transfer. Version 2. Effective from 2026-09-01. Audience: customers. Banana Bank is a simulated bank: this document describes only the policies of the test environment, in euros. Balances and movements come from the customer's bank ledger, never from this document.

## Answer

A standard internal transfer between simulator accounts has a EUR 0 fee. This is separate from the monthly account fee of the product.
